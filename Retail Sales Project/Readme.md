# RETAIL SALES ANALYSIS - SUMMARY

## Data Cleaning and Preparation
* For accurate time-based analysis, the Date column was converted to the correct date format and additional fields created, including Month Name, Quarter, and Weekend Flag.
* Validated transaction accuracy by verifying that Quantity × Price per Unit matched the Total Amount for each record.
* Created customer Age Group categories: Young Adults (18–25), Millennials (26–35), Gen X (36–45), Boomers (46–55), and Seniors (56–65).
* Classified products into pricing tiers: Budget, Mid-Range, and Premium.

## Key findings

 ### Best category
Electronics emerged as the primary revenue driver, generating $156,905 in revenue, outperforming the Clothing category by a narrow margin of just over $1,000. Electronics had a higher efficiency which was demonstrated by a higher Average Order Value (AOV). This indicates that while volume was lower than Clothing, the Electronics category yielded a higher value per individual sale.

 ### Customer demographic insights
The customer base is relatively balanced indicating that the products appeal across genders without a strong bias. Boomers are the leading customers in terms of revenue and representation while Gen X and millennials follow closely. The young age group is the least represented and contributes the lowest revenue among all age groups.
Overall the business appears to rely more on established higher purchasing power age groups presenting an opportunity to strengthen engagement with the young age group for long term business growth

### One actionable recommendation with data proof.
Based on the revenue by quarter trend, there is a need to reinforce targeted marketing effort in quarter three due to the revenue dip evidenced in the analysis.
